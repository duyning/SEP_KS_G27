# HL-RCS Business Rules

| ID | Rule | Rule statement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **BR-01** | Public registration | Public accounts must be registered through a verified Google identity and start with the `USER` role. `STAFF` and `ADMIN` accounts may be provisioned only by the system or an authorised Admin. |
| **BR-02** | Ownership and permissions | A Room Host may manage only properties they own and room types and rooms within those properties. The system must derive the acting account from the authenticated session. Staff and Admin actions must follow the permission matrix for the relevant workflow. No account may grant itself privileges or decide its own application. For member features, Platform Members are accounts with the `USER` or `ROOM_HOST` role. |
| **BR-03** | Property registration and drafts | An active Room Host may save incomplete form data as a browser draft and submit a property they are authorised to register. An Admin may submit through the same authorised owner workflow. Initial submission requires the property name, type, description, manager name, contact phone, area or commune, address, agreement to terms, 1–10 photos, proof of ownership or management rights, ID and fire-safety evidence. If map coordinates are supplied, both must be valid. The applicant must select photos and documents again when reopening a draft. |
| **BR-04** | Property verification decision | Only active Staff eligible under the property verification queue policy may approve, reject or request more information. A Staff member may not decide a submission they filed or a property they own or manage. Admin has oversight access. Approval verifies the property and creates or updates its approved profile. The Room Host role is granted through BR-31. |
| **BR-05** | Private property evidence | The submitting account, Staff within the current verification scope and an oversight Admin may access property verification documents through authorised workflows. Any support-related Staff access requires an active assignment and a demonstrated need for that evidence. Every view or download must recheck the account’s scope and the document’s association with the submission. Verification evidence must remain private. |
| **BR-06** | Verification and moderation history | Every decision must record the actor, target, time and outcome. Rejection and requests for additional information or changes require a reason. Property verification and content moderation must retain separate decision records. |
| **BR-07** | Public accommodation and content | A public property must have an approved verification, be active, have an active owner and have no outstanding re-verification restriction. Public room types must be active and belong to an eligible property. Separate rental listings, where supported, and community posts require publication approval. Public services must satisfy BR-33. Each read must apply the eligibility rules for the relevant record, including media, search, details, wishlist, comparison and AI results. |
| **BR-08** | Rent, area, capacity and fees | Rent and floor area must be greater than zero. Capacity must be a positive integer. Fees and deposits must be non-negative. Every displayed amount must have a currency or unit and a charging basis where applicable. |
| **BR-09** | Property re-verification | Submitting a change to the approved address, map coordinates, designated manager, ownership or management evidence, or identity document must hide the property and its dependent public accommodation records and remove verified status from public display. Rejection and requests for more information keep the property hidden. Only approval of the valid current proposal may clear the re-verification restriction. For routine content edits, the approved version remains public while the update is reviewed. |
| **BR-10** | Room transfer details | A room transfer post must identify the accommodation via a linked property or room type, or provide its full address when unlisted. It must include a contact and an earliest handover date on or after the submission or resubmission date in Vietnam local time. |
| **BR-11** | Room type visibility and vacancies | Inactive room types are excluded from public discovery, details and comparisons. A publicly eligible room type with zero vacancies remains visible in general search, detail and comparison with a **No available rooms** label. It is excluded from vacancy-only filters, AI recommendations and new viewing requests. |
| **BR-12** | Wishlist ownership and uniqueness | An active Platform Member may save a publicly eligible room type at most once in their own wishlist. A wishlist entry references a room type. Only the owning account may view and change its wishlist. |
| **BR-13** | Wishlist availability changes | A saved room type remains saved when it becomes full and must show its current availability. If it becomes ineligible for public access, the account may see its own saved reference while restricted accommodation details remain hidden. |
| **BR-14** | Room type comparison | An active Platform Member may compare two or three distinct, publicly eligible room types saved in their own wishlist, including full types and types at different properties. Compare current price, fees and availability. Label missing values **Not provided**. Remove or replace ineligible selections before comparing. |
| **BR-15** | Accommodation review eligibility | An active Platform Member may submit a review supported by a rental agreement or Room Host confirmation identifying the renter, property and rental period. Staff assigned to verify the evidence must approve the review before publication. Evidence is private to the author, eligible Staff and oversight Admin. A viewing, saved room type or service interaction alone is insufficient evidence. |
| **BR-16** | Accommodation review lifecycle | Each member may have at most one non-deleted review per property. The review may reference a room type. Only its author may edit or delete it. A rating or text edit returns the review to pending and hides it until reapproved. Deletion hides it publicly and preserves its processing history. |
| **BR-17** | Community and rental content types | Each community post has exactly one type: `PASS_ROOM`, `ROOMMATE` or `PASS_ITEM`. Community posts cannot create property, room type or individual room records. If separate `RENTAL_LISTING` content is supported, only the authorised Room Host may create it for a property or room type they manage. |
| **BR-18** | Community post lifecycle | An active Platform Member may submit a community post. Only the author may edit, close or delete it. The post stays private until approved under BR-41. Changing published content or contact details must trigger review again and hide the post during review. Closed or deleted posts are hidden from search and public details. Their processing history remains available to authorised staff. |
| **BR-19** | AI recommendations | Recommendations must reference existing publicly eligible room types with at least one vacancy. Prices, addresses, amenities and availability must come from system records. If no room type meets the recommendation criteria, return an empty result. |
| **BR-20** | Distance calculation | Calculate straight-line distance in kilometres from one selected origin to each property’s approved coordinates. The member may select an origin or grant permission to use device location. Search results, details and comparisons must use the same origin and method. Show **Distance unavailable** when the required coordinates are missing. External travel directions are independent of this estimate. |
| **BR-21** | In-system notifications | Create notifications for successful property, service application, post and accommodation review decisions, viewing and service request submissions and responses, viewing cancellations, zero-vacancy alerts, support updates and report resolutions. Decisions go to the applicant or author, requests to the responsible host or Service Owner, and responses to the requester. Each event may create at most one notification per recipient. Only the recipient may access that notification. |
| **BR-22** | Viewing cancellation | A requester may cancel their own `PENDING` or `APPROVED` viewing before its scheduled start, measured in Vietnam local time. The system must record `CANCELLED`, the actor and cancellation time, preserve the history and notify the managing Room Host. Cancellation leaves charges and room availability unchanged. |
| **BR-23** | Price disclosure | Room Hosts must disclose monthly rent, deposit, applicable fees, charging units and known conditions. Service Owners must select **Fixed price**, **Starting from** or **Quotation required**. Fixed and starting prices must be positive. Quotation-based prices have no numeric amount. Conditional fees must state when they apply. Members may report price discrepancies under BR-44. |
| **BR-24** | Community submission limit | An account may first submit at most five community posts in a rolling 24-hour period. Count the first successful submission of each post by server time, including posts later rejected, closed or deleted. Drafts, edits and resubmissions consume no additional allowance. |
| **BR-25** | Viewings and room inventory | Viewing requests leave room inventory unchanged and create no reservation. A drop to zero vacancies must preserve existing request states and notify the host and affected requesters. An approved appointment remains valid and can be cancelled under BR-22. The host may reject a pending request with a reason, and zero vacancies block new requests. |
| **BR-26** | Inactive accounts | An inactive or blocked account must be denied protected actions through both new sign-ins and existing sessions. |
| **BR-27** | Google identity uniqueness | A verified Google subject may identify only one HL-RCS account. Signing in again must use the associated account and preserve its privileges. Matching email alone must never grant access to a separate account. |
| **BR-28** | Physical rooms and vacancy counts | Every physical room must belong to exactly one property and one room type within that property. Room numbers must be unique within a property. The available count must be derived from physical rooms in the `VACANT` state. |
| **BR-29** | Availability confirmation | An authorised confirmation of the entire room type records the actor and server timestamp. Adding or removing a room, moving it between room types or changing its occupancy invalidates confirmation for the affected types. Without a valid confirmation, show **Not yet confirmed**. More than seven elapsed days after confirmation, show **Availability not confirmed recently**. These indicators leave room statuses, vacancy counts and public eligibility unchanged. |
| **BR-30** | Viewing eligibility and details | An active Platform Member may request a viewing of a publicly eligible room type with at least one vacancy. Recheck eligibility when the request is submitted. Record the requester, contact name and phone, property, room type, managing Room Host and a future date and time in Vietnam local time. The request applies to the room type. |
| **BR-31** | Room Host application | An active `USER` with valid profile contact details may apply once for the `ROOM_HOST` role. Active Staff or Admin may approve, request changes or reject the current pending application, subject to BR-06. The applicant may resubmit the same application when permitted. Approval grants `ROOM_HOST`. Each property still requires verification under BR-03 and BR-04. |
| **BR-32** | Property review scope and resubmission | The current property verification policy determines whether eligible Staff work in a shared queue or must hold a current assignment. In assignment mode, Staff access ends when the assignment ends. Decisions must use the current review version. Applicants may resubmit under the applicable edit policy and must keep changes within the fields requested when the review specifies a field scope. |
| **BR-33** | Service permission and publication | Service Owner is an independent `MANAGE_SERVICE` permission that leaves the account role unchanged. Public service details and media require an active service, an active provider, active `MANAGE_SERVICE` permission, and an approved application with both permission and listing decisions approved. |
| **BR-34** | Service application and uniqueness | An active authenticated account may apply with applicant name, service name, category, description, area served, contact phone, a pricing model under BR-23 and 1–5 verification documents. Before receiving the permission, the account may have only one application in progress. An active Service Owner may manage multiple services. Within a provider, a service name cannot duplicate a non-deleted service or an open request for the same name. |
| **BR-35** | Service application review | Any active Staff or Admin may review the current pending service application without an assignment. Applicants may not decide their own applications. The system must record separate permission and listing decisions, apply BR-06 and require a reason of 10–2,000 characters for rejection or requested supplementation. A listing may be approved only when permission is approved. |
| **BR-36** | Service update and deletion | Only an active Service Owner may propose an update or deletion to their own service. Each service may have at most one open change request. The proposal must reference the current service version and give a reason. Until approval, the previous approved version remains the basis for public display. Rejection leaves it in place. Approved deletion hides that service, preserves history and leaves the permission and other services unchanged. |
| **BR-37** | Service application supplementation | Only the applicant may resubmit the current version of a `NEEDS_SUPPLEMENT` application. They must provide a response to the request and retain or replace supporting files as required. Resubmission returns the same application to `PENDING` for review. |
| **BR-38** | Service evidence and photos | Verification documents and proposal images are private to the applicant and authorised active Staff or Admin. A proposal may include up to five JPG, PNG or WebP photos, each up to 5 MB. Public images must come from the approved version of an eligible service under BR-33. |
| **BR-39** | Viewing request processing | A member may have one pending request per room type and may update their own pending request. They may schedule up to 30 calendar days ahead in Vietnam local time and may not request a viewing of their own property. Only the managing Room Host may approve or reject a pending request. Approval requires a future appointment and a vacancy at decision time. Rejection requires a reason. Record who decided and when. |
| **BR-40** | Comments on community posts | Comments and replies are publicly readable only on approved posts. Active authenticated accounts may comment or reply. Only the comment author may edit it. The author, Staff or Admin may delete it. Deleting a root comment removes its replies. |
| **BR-41** | Community moderation | Active Staff or Admin may approve, reject or request changes to the current pending revision of a post, subject to BR-06. Authors cannot moderate their own posts. The author may revise and resubmit a post marked as needing changes. Admin controls whether AI moderation is off, advisory or automatic. Under automatic moderation, uncertain or safety-flagged results and processing failures must go to human review. |
| **BR-42** | Account status administration | An active Admin may activate or deactivate another account. A status change must be audited and invalidate the affected account’s existing sessions. An Admin may not change their own account status. |
| **BR-43** | Property assistance | Only Staff assigned to an open assistance request may enter or change information within its authorised scope. Each action must record the actor, request and time. Access to protected evidence ends when the assignment ends. The requester receives status updates under BR-21. |
| **BR-44** | Report handling | Active Platform Members may report a record or a price discrepancy with a reason. An authorised handler must record the outcome and rationale. Admin handles price discrepancies. The reporter receives the resolution under BR-21. |
| **BR-45** | In-platform service requests | If the project includes in-platform requests, an active Platform Member may send a request for a publicly eligible service. Only the requester and the responsible Service Owner may see its details and respond in their respective roles. Request submission and provider response must follow BR-21. |
