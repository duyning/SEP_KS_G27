Scope and interpretation
Platform Member denotes an ordinary authenticated USER, not a fifth persisted role and not a generalization that grants all USER-only permissions to STAFF/ADMIN.
All IDs are local to this diagram and do not renumber the Word catalogue. Main operations are grouped; children are optional extensions, not a chronological sequence.
Account registration belongs to the guest-to-member journey. Existing members sign in with password or Google and may sign out.
Host and service-provider management after approval are outside this diagram. MANAGE_SERVICE is a capability independent of account role.

Evidence and constraints
UC-PM01: AccommodationController, AccommodationRecommendationService, GeminiAccommodationClient; public search/detail, map UI, AI-assisted criteria. FavoriteController limits favorites to USER/ROOM_HOST. FavoritesPage and RoomComparisonPage implement comparison (up to four selections).
UC-PM02: ViewingController and ViewingService. Create/list/detail for USER/ROOM_HOST; editing and cancellation require an owned PENDING appointment. No member-side approval or payment.
UC-PM03: CommunityController/feed, CommunityCommentController/Service, CommunityReportController/Service. Published feed, comments/replies, author-scoped comment edits/removal, private own report tracking.
UC-PM04: CommunityController and CommunityService. Create with media, list own review state, resubmit under service checks, delete own posts. CommunityPostRequest accepts ROOMMATE, ROOM_TRANSFER and SECOND_HAND categories.
UC-PM05: PublicServiceController, ServiceRequestController/Service, ServiceRatingController/Service. Requests require a public service, future schedule and a different provider. Member tracks requests; provider accepts/rejects. One rating per author/service, no self-rating. No member request-cancellation endpoint.
UC-PM06: ServiceComplaintController/Service. Submit evidence, inspect own complaints and outcomes. Explanation requests go to the service provider, not to the reporter.
UC-PM07: HostOnboardingController/Service and frontend become-host flow. USER submits account information plus first property dossier; approval can grant ROOM_HOST. Supplementation uses ownership and state checks. Legacy HostApplicationController remains, but is not shown as a second main onboarding journey.
UC-PM08: ServiceRegistrationController/Service. Authenticated applicant submits first CREATE listing, tracks decisions, supplements NEEDS_SUPPLEMENT, cancels PENDING with revision. Later provider listing management is outside scope.
UC-PM09: RentalContractSigningController and RentalContractService.openAsTenant/signAsTenant. Valid unexpired signing token, contract/party checks and verified matching tenant identity are required.
UC-PM10: AuthController/AuthServiceImpl and PhoneVerificationController/Service. Own profile/password rules and SMS phone verification. SMS provider depends on runtime configuration.
UC-PM11: CollectedPostController.publicFeed; read published collected posts, without import or moderation authority.
UC-PM12: NoticeController/Service, CommunityNotificationController, ViewingController notifications; own notification feeds and read-state updates.
UC-PM13: AuthController and GoogleBrowserSignInController, AuthServiceImpl. Password/Google authentication and sign-out; no password-reset workflow inferred.
