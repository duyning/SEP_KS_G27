IDs are local to these diagrams; they do not renumber the Word catalogue.
Source: src/main/java/org/hlrcs/controller and service.
AdminOverviewController/Service: dashboard, accounts, status, assignments overview and activity (no personnel creation).
StaffController, StaffService, PropertyVerificationAccess: property inspection versus Staff-only decisions, assignment/version/conflict checks.
HostApplicationController/Service: retained standalone role applications, distinct from combined property onboarding.
CommunityModerationController, CommunityService, CommunityCommentService: post review and comment deletion; Admin-only automatic moderation settings.
CommunityReportController/Service, CommunityReportAiController/Assistant: Admin-only report resolution and optional configured AI analysis.
ServiceReviewController/Service: shared service reviews, separate capability/listing decisions, supplementation; no assignment prerequisite.
ServiceComplaintController/Service: impartial Staff/Admin decisions, DISMISS/REMOVE or requesting the provider response.
CollectedPostController/Service: collection worker, publication and deletion.
AuthController/AuthServiceImpl, NoticeController/Service: own profile, credentials, sessions, recipient-scoped notices and Admin announcements.
AI analysis is advisory; the administrator resolves community reports. The collection worker is the actual external integration, not an invented payment/email service.
No Admin-to-Staff actor generalization: Admin cannot perform property-verification decisions.
